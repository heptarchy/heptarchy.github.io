Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy describes privacy on this Heptarchy Studio website and when you contact us. For questions, email hiencc18032@gmail.com.

01 — Scope of this policy

This policy covers this studio website and communications you choose to send to Heptarchy Studio. Individual games may process different information. A game’s own privacy notice and in-app disclosures should be consulted for its specific data practices; this website policy does not describe or authorize undisclosed game data collection.

02 — Information you provide

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you include. Please share only what is needed for your inquiry and avoid sending passwords, payment card information, or other sensitive information.

03 — Website data and cookies

This page contains no contact form, account registration, advertising SDK, analytics script, tracking pixel, or third-party font. Its code does not set cookies or use browser storage. When hosted online, the hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser details, access times, and requested pages, to deliver and secure the website. Provider practices depend on the hosting service used.

04 — How information is used and shared

Information you send by email is used to respond to your request, provide support, and follow up on the matter you raised. Email and hosting providers process information as part of providing their services. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or protection of rights. This page has no mechanism for selling personal information or sharing it for targeted advertising.

05 — Retention and security

Support correspondence may be retained while an inquiry is being handled and as needed for relevant records or obligations. To ask about retention or request deletion of correspondence, contact us using the email below. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

06 — Your choices and requests

You can browse this page without submitting personal information. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us, email hiencc18032@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request.” Identify the relevant message or game without including unnecessary sensitive data. Requests will be assessed according to applicable requirements, and verification may be needed before information is disclosed or changed.

07 — Children’s privacy

This studio website does not include accounts or forms for collecting children’s information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to us can contact us to request review and deletion. Age suitability and any child-specific data practices for a game must be described in that game’s own notice.

08 — Games, third parties, and external links

Games may have separate features, permissions, advertising, analytics, purchase services, or online functions. The actual providers, data categories, purposes, retention, and controls must be disclosed for each game before this policy is used as that game’s privacy notice. No particular game SDK or provider is represented as being used here. Following an email link opens your email application, whose provider applies its own privacy terms.

09 — Changes and contact

Updates to this website policy will appear on this page with a revised date. For privacy questions or support, contact Heptarchy Studio at hiencc18032@gmail.com.

Website privacy notice. Game-specific privacy disclosures are provided separately for each applicable title.
